## 1. Common rules (no new threshold)

- **ORS (offset-subtracted retest share).** From the difference d_u of unit u, subtract the median of d over the other units of the same bundle (excluding itself); if |d_u − o_{−u}| ≤ c·√2·s_u, the unit is at retest level. If the share is 0.90 or more (the same as the integer form `analysis.retest_level`), «at retest level by location adjustment». If there are fewer than 10 units, or more than 10 % of units are subtracted, no verdict is possible. c · s follow the rule of manuscript 3.4 for each dataset: Tong — t₀.₉₇₅(df) and the per-unit SD of the c10 repeats; ON-Harmony — 1.96 and the per-unit SD of the OXF1PRI repeats (2 persons × 6 sessions); Wong — 1.96 and the SD of the repeat series (TE62R2 · TE132R2) after subtracting the mean difference for each repeat pair. When the scale is changed, the SD of that scale from the same repeats.
- **ΔTE* (TE tolerance).** The ΔTE at which the expected ORS becomes 0.90 for offset-subtracted differences ~ N(0, 2s²(1 + 1/(n−1)) + ΔTE²τ²). τ is the SD of the TE slopes between units (with the noise variance subtracted). If the root exceeds 70 ms (the TE span of the data), «≥ 70 ms».
- **Reading of S2.** If the change of the group difference between sites in the fit results ÷ equation (7) (including the T1 term) is within 0.80–1.25 in all relevant cells, «equation (7) holds in the fit results». The range 0.80–1.25 is taken by convention from the range used in average bioequivalence practice (the principle of judging equivalence by an interval: Schuirmann 1987, J Pharmacokinet Biopharm 15:657–680, doi:10.1007/BF01068419 — this reference did not set ±20 %).
- **Scale comparison (E4).** The ORS difference between two scales on the same units and a paired bootstrap 95 % interval (if there are two or more participants, the participants are redrawn, otherwise the units · B 2000 · seed 0). And the residual sum of squares of two one-parameter models, «constant raw shift» versus «constant logit shift» (d = expit(logit ν_A + c) − ν_A).
- **Descriptive (no verdict).** Proportional bias (Bland & Altman 1999: the slope of the regression of the difference on the mean) · Spearman ρ · rank-reversal share (among pairs of units farther apart than c·√2·s in A).

## 3. Real data (ROI values already computed · no images opened)

**E1 Wong (3 persons × 27 units × 8 TEs).** For each participant, the x · y TE slope k̂ of the units (OLS over the 8 TEs · repeat series excluded) and the 8-TE mean x̄. σ = repeat SD after subtracting the mean difference (for x · y separately) · noise of var(k̂) = σ²/4200 ms² · noise of var(x̄) = σ²/8. τ̂ = √max(var(k̂) − σ²/4200, 0), β̂ = cov(k̂, x̄)/(var(x̄) − σ²/8). **E2 ON-Harmony (19 pairs of scanners that can be judged).** For each of six scales (raw NDI · x · raw FISO · logit FISO · y · ODI), the original share (no offset) and the ORS of the pair summary (median over common participants of B − A · per unit, `analysis.pair_summary`), with s the OXF1PRI repeat SD of the same scale · c 1.96. **E3 Tong (3 persons × 27 units × 45 center pairs).** For each of six scales, the original share (x · y · ODI must equal the reproduced values) and the ORS (the offset is the median excluding itself over the 27 units for each participant × center pair) — c = t₀.₉₇₅(df) · s is the per-unit SD of that scale in the c10 repeats.

**E4 Scale comparison.** Main analysis: Wong 62 versus 132 ms — the ORS difference and its interval for x versus raw NDI, y versus logit FISO (81 units · participant bundles), and for each participant the residual sums of squares of the two models (constant raw shift · constant logit shift) and c. ## 6. Fix before computing (after S65 commit 03631a0 · before computing any new quantity · 2026-11-14)

**Fix 1 — main analysis of S2.** In the design fixed in section 2 (20 persons per site and group, the L1 relaxation variation drawn once for each person and held fixed, and only the ROI noise changed by Monte Carlo), the change of the group difference between sites is mixed with «the mean difference of the relaxation of the persons drawn». The standard deviation of that quantity, estimated algebraically, is about 0.026 logit (SD of the shift of one person about 0.068 at TE 100 ms · mean of 20 persons · difference of four cells), which is the same size as the value of equation (7) (Figure 3 scenario −0.148 · L2 two compartments about −0.019). Left as it is, the ratio 0.80–1.25 would fail through sampling error unrelated to the fitter. Therefore the following fix is made before computing.
- **Main analysis = population mode:** all simulated participants have the group-center relaxation (the «population-level estimate» setting of Proposition 4). The Monte Carlo ROI noise is unchanged. The reading of the ratio (0.80–1.25) and the main numbers are for this mode only.
- **Auxiliary = original design (L1 variation):** computed as it is, but the comparison target is the algebraic value computed with the relaxation of the same persons (the error of drawing cancels) · only the difference is reported and no ratio is given.
- Code: `analyses.s2` · `s2_subjects` · `s2_sample_algebra` (two tests added — the population mode has one central person · the algebra for the same persons equals equation (7) when the relaxation is all at the center).
