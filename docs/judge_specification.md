## 0. What cannot be done — written first
- The algebraic result (SPEC_v2 §1 · stage 2 X1) is **information-theoretic non-identifiability**: with single-TE data, per-compartment relaxation reweighting and a change in compartment fractions are the same operation, so no estimator — ML included — can tell the two apart with those data.
- If ML appears to have resolved this, it is returning the training distribution (the prior). [literature] Gyori NG, Palombo M, Clark CA, Zhang H, Alexander DC. Training data distribution significantly impacts the estimation of tissue microstructure with machine learning. *Magn Reson Med* 2022;87(2):932–947. doi:10.1002/mrm.29014 — bibliographic details checked against the repository record (UCL Discovery · Cardiff ORCA) · the body text could not be checked.
- **Prohibited:** do not write «restored the tissue difference · removed the relaxation · harmonized». **ML point estimates are not used as results.** The results are only the shrinkage · width · calibration status of the posterior distribution.

## 3. Estimation problem and simulator
### 3-1. ROI unit · quantity of interest · observation · condition (relay ZZZe-2)
- **ROI unit:** one pair of ROI-level observations of one ROI of one person, measured at two sites A · B.
- **Quantity of interest θ\* (6 dimensions):** Δ = (ΔNDI, ΔFISO, ΔODI) = tissue_B − tissue_A (true fractions · natural units) + tissue_A = (NDI_A, FISO_A, ODI_A). [design] Reason for estimating tissue_A jointly: the identifiability of Δ depends on the reference tissue state (SG2 · table above).
- **Observation x:** C3 arm — logit transform of the ROI mean of the F2 output (NDI · FISO · ODI) of the two sites (6 dimensions). C7 arm — plus MD · FA · per-shell mean signal (7 per site · 14 dimensions in total — same coordinates as `decomp/stage1/lib.py:15` `coords(…, "C7")`). The two arms are trained separately from the same simulation.
- **Condition a (one judge handles all of it):** ① acquisition — TE_A · TE_B · TR_A · TR_B · direction design (S/V) · b value of b0 (0/5) · number of b0 · coil (32/64) · SNR_A · SNR_B · N_eff ② relaxation scenario — L level label · s_T2 · ρ · s_T1 · diffuse shift (T2 · T1 · allocation) · lesion multipliers (m_en · m_in · m_T1) ③ generator assumption — tortuosity convention (T-in / T-app / T-none) · T1 scenario (i inside > outside · ii outside > inside · iii equal).
  [design] The relaxation scenario and the generator assumption are entered as **conditions**, not as priors — one judge produces L0–L3 and the per-assumption results, so there is no retraining with changed priors (consistent with «S4 1 time»). **Verdict and classification** use the **conditional posterior** p(θ\* | x, a, k) for each generator assumption k (three tortuosity × three T1 scenarios = nine) (§5 · §7 · §8 — relay ZZZg-1). The uniform mixture of the nine assumes «x has no information about k» and so is not the Bayesian marginal posterior — it is reported **for reference only** and is not used for verdict · classification.
- **Nuisance variables (marginalized):** compartment T2 · T1 (distribution set by the scenario) · tissue d∥ (v3 range) · within-ROI heterogeneity · noise · fibre direction.
- **Domain of application (author decision E2):** two shells (b ≈ 1000 · 2000) · direction design S (= recorded bval · bvec of P2a · OXF1PRI) · V (= P2b · NOT1ACH) — `data/protocols/` (R10 exception 1 · access log `prereg/artifacts/decomp/onharmony_access_log.json`) · TE 60 – 110 ms · TR 3.0 – 11.0 s. **Outside the domain:** Tong (b 3000) · Wongkornchaovalit (b 700) · MUSHAC — only the algebraic card is used. Reason: only designs with an actual bvec can be simulated exactly, and widening the domain dilutes training at the same budget.

### 3-2. Priors of tissue and Δ [design]
- **tissue_A:** **continuous uniform** over the tissue range of the v3 generator (dense table `TISSUE_E5` — NDI 0.30 – 0.80 · FISO 0.01 – 0.30 · ODI grid 0.08 · 0.20 · 0.40 — `decomp/stage1/config.py` · SPEC_v2 §4): NDI U[0.30, 0.80] · FISO U[0.01, 0.30] · ODI U[0.08, 0.40].
- **Main reported prior of Δ:** ΔNDI U(±0.10) · ΔODI U(±0.10) · ΔFISO U(±0.05) · components independent. [literature→design] As a literature basis for the width, a multicentre NODDI reproducibility study (Andica et al. 2020 · coefficient of variation 2.3 – 14 % between scanners of two vendors) was found, but the original text (publisher · PMC) was blocked by authentication · verification screens and could not be checked — it is left as [design]. Rationale (design): at NDI ≈ 0.6, 14 % is about 0.08, so ±0.10 covers the size of the between-scanner difference.
- **Training prior of Δ:** **twice the width** of the main prior (ΔNDI · ΔODI U(±0.20) · ΔFISO U(±0.10)) — both the narrow prior (×0.5) and the wide prior (×2) are produced by reweighting (§5 · §7 iii).
- **Boundary handling:** draw (tissue_A, Δ), and if tissue_B = tissue_A + Δ falls outside the range, draw again (rejection). The actual training prior density is therefore the **truncated uniform** of «A range × Δ training width × B within range», and the main prior is uniform with the same truncation → the importance weight is «constant inside the main width · 0 outside» [algebra]. The prior variance Var_prior(uᵀΔ) is computed from the main prior (with truncation) with Monte Carlo 10⁶ samples.

### 3-6. ROI-level observation model — the key to resolving the saturation of stages 2 · 3 (relay ZZZe-3 M · ZZZf-1)
1. At one site, **K = 256** voxels are generated (ZZZf-1). Tissue of each voxel = ROI centre + within-ROI heterogeneity — NDI · ODI SD 0.03 · FISO SD 0.02 · clipped to widened ranges (NDI [0.25, 0.85] · FISO [0.0, 0.35] · ODI [0.04, 0.50] — the ranges of the compartment signal table) [design — rationale: values larger than the between-person apparent NDI SD of stage 3 X13 (0.006 – 0.016) and larger than the tissue grid cell spacing (NDI 0.02) · written down as a limitation without a sensitivity arm].
2. Fibre direction: independent uniform per site over the 8 fibre directions of the v1 grid (`config.FIBRE_DIRS` — deterministic set on the hemisphere · SPEC_v1 §6) — the head position of a travelling subject differs per session and NODDI parameters are rotation invariant, so direction is a nuisance variable · the v1 set is used to avoid creating a new factor [design]. Voxels within an ROI have the same direction (dispersion is handled by ODI).
   Compartment signals: a table (tissue d∥ 5 · ODI grid · d⊥/d∥ grid · direction 8) is built once per design with the AMICO synthesis function and linearly interpolated per voxel — at the grid points of the table the values must equal those of `synth.Proto` (known-answer test).
3. The signal is synthesized per voxel (compartment functions of `decomp/stage1/synth.py` · same units), noise is added, and it is then fitted with F2. The C7 arm also computes DTI and shell means (`synth.ext_coords`).
4. The mean m and covariance Σ̂ of the K fitted values are computed.
5. ROI observation: **x = m + ε · ε ~ N(0, Σ̂ (1/N_eff − 1/K))** · N_eff U_log[16, 200] (ZZZf-1) · always N_eff ≤ K.
- Rationale: the noise bias of the fitter (nonlinear) enters exactly through the K-voxel Monte Carlo, and the remaining ROI-mean noise is added by the central limit. The order is the same as in the actual pipeline — per-voxel fit → ROI mean (the signal is not averaged first). N_eff does not use ON-Harmony values and is covered by a range. The card is produced at representative values {25, 50, 100}.

## 4. Estimator — neural posterior estimation (NPE · relay ZZZe-4)
- Library: sbi 0.27.0 in the lock (torch 2.14.0) — 0 additional installations (the features needed — NSF · ensemble · SBC · TARP · C2ST — are in the installed version · confirmed 2026-10-24). If a missing feature is needed, stop condition 1.
- Architecture [design]: neural spline flow (NSF) 5 layers · hidden 128 · embedding MLP receiving condition a and observation x together (3 layers · 128) · learning rate 5e-4 · batch 256 · stop if the validation loss stalls for 20 epochs · maximum 500 epochs.

- Two arms: C3 ensemble · C7 ensemble (same simulation).
- Two auxiliary models: oracle NPE (§7 ii) · observation density flow q(x | a) (§7 v).
- Frozen: all weights · normalization constants · mixture rules are committed with their sha256.

## 5. Identifiability figures — definitions (relay ZZZe-5)
- **Shrinkage of one observation:** c_u(x, a, k) = 1 − Var_post(uᵀΔ | x, a, k) / Var_prior(uᵀΔ) — per generator assumption k (ZZZg-1). The posterior is importance-reweighted to the main prior (§3-2 — only samples inside the main width remain) · the number of posterior samples is in §13 14 (after ZZZg — formerly 10,000) · if the samples in the main support do not reach the target number, «reweighting impossible» (§13 1).
- **Design-level shrinkage (figure on the card):** C_u(a, k) = median of c_u(·, k) over the M = 100 observations simulated under that condition — computed from the acquisition record alone, without real data. The card · map give C · σ · MDE for each of the nine k, **the classification uses the worst of the nine** (lowest C · largest MDE), and the median of the nine is written with it (ZZZg-1).
- **Absolute units:** σ_u(a) = median of the posterior SD · detectable difference MDE_u = 2.80 × σ_u (two-sided 5 % · approximate power 80 %). **The representative figures on the card give MDE · C together (author decision E3).** Form that clinical researchers read: «Between these two sites, an NDI difference smaller than X cannot be distinguished from relaxation.»
- **Direction u:** e_NDI · e_FISO · e_ODI · u_relax (the (ΔNDI, ΔFISO) direction of the relaxation shift that the TE · TR difference of that pair produces at the reference tissue — the shift in linearized coordinates (SPEC_v1 §1-1) moved to fraction units with the Jacobian [algebra] · computed for each test case at the true tissue_A of that case) · u_⊥ (orthogonal to u_relax).
- **Classification [design]:** identified C ≥ 0.75 (posterior SD ≤ 1/2 of prior SD) · non-identified C < 0.25 · partial in between — on the card · map this is applied to the lowest C of the nine k. Sensitivity: the classification with thresholds (0.5, 0.1) is reported as well. Additional labels: «prior-dependent» · «assumption-dependent» (§7 iii) · «no verdict possible» (strata that failed in §7 i · §7 v).

## 6. Comparison baseline — linear-Gaussian (Fisher) identifiability (relay ZZZe-6)
- From the same simulation (new factors · new data 0): ① for each condition a and reference tissue, estimate the Jacobian J by local linear regression of the ROI observation mean μ(θ\*) on θ\* ② Fisher information with the observation covariance and the contribution of the nuisance variables ③ eliminate the nuisance variables with the Schur complement ④ combine with a Gaussian prior for the posterior variance → C_u^lin.
- **Comparison partner (relay ZZZi v3-2):** C^lin stays with k marginalized — dividing the 100 observations per cell among the nine k gives about 11 per cell · k, too few for a 6-dimensional regression. C^lin is therefore compared **only with the uniform mixture of the NPE (reference value · §13 1)** — both sides make the same assumption, «the information x gives about k is not used». It is not compared with per-k results or with the result of the worst k (that would compare things under different assumptions).
- Report: «the difference between C and C^lin under the same assumption (no k information)» — for each cell of the card · map, C of the uniform mixture · C^lin · their difference (C_mix − C^lin) side by side · the difference map is also produced only from this pair.
- Interpretation rule (set in advance): the difference is reported only as the size of the nonlinearity (SG1 · SG2). The main result is the NPE (per k) that passed the calibration check (§7 i) · C^lin and the uniform mixture are not subject to the calibration check.

## 7. Five criteria fixed before computing — test · threshold · verdict (relay ZZZe-7)
- **Strata:** acquisition type 5 (match · TE only · TR only · TE+TR · TE+TR+design) × L level 4 (L0–L3) = **20 strata** · test data 1,000 per stratum (new seed).
### (i) Calibration — core verdict
- For each test case, 1,000 posterior samples → central credible intervals per Δ component at 50 · 80 · 90 · 95 %.
- **Posterior:** the verdict uses the **conditional posterior at the true k** of the test case (mixture of the five in the ensemble) (ZZZg-1). Because the test data drew θ from the main prior (`decomp/s4/plan.py:58`), the verdict uses the posterior reweighted to the main prior (confirmed in ZZZg-3).
- **Pass:** for each stratum × component × level, empirical inclusion rate ≥ nominal − 3·√(p(1 − p)/n) — n is the **number of cases actually remaining** in that stratum (excluding reweighting-impossible cases · ZZZg-5 · e.g. n = 1,000 · at 90 %, 0.872). Overcalibration (above nominal + 0.05) is marked «conservative».
- Diagnostics (not pass criteria): SBC rank histograms · TARP joint inclusion rate.
- **Verdict:** failed strata are «no verdict possible» on the card. **If 10 or more of the 20 strata fail, S4 as a whole fails.**
### (ii) Prior leakage — comparison with the reference posterior distribution
- Oracle setting: observation = apparent signal fractions (f_in · f_iso) and ODI without the fitter + Gaussian noise σ_o = 0.005 (per site) [design]. The oracle NPE is trained with the same prior · conditions (a single model is allowed).
- Reference posterior (changed by ZZZg-2): 100 cases per acquisition type · **MCMC on the potential of exact Gaussian likelihood + prior** — θ\* and the nuisance variables (the three relaxation variables of A · if L1, the three z · z′) are sampled together and only θ\* is kept (9 – 12 dimensions) · slice sampling · 4 chains (starting points drawn dispersed from the prior) · 500 discarded per chain · 2,500 kept. Convergence: split R̂ ≤ 1.01 · bulk ESS ≥ 500 for each component of θ\* — cases that fall short are dropped and their number is reported. **If more than 20 % of the 500 cases drop out, (ii) is «no verdict possible» — S4 is not treated as passed and stops as «technically impossible».** Implementation in §13 7.
  - ESS of the old method (importance sampling of 10⁶ prior samples) — reference check (2026-10-25 · reference cases 0 – 4 · all match stratum · unrelated to the NPE): L0 3.92 · L3 1.70 · L1 1.00 · L1 2.60 · L2 1.69 (out of 10⁶ samples) — far below the threshold 500 (same as the estimate in review session error 174).
- **Pass — all three:** (a) in the TR only · TE+TR strata, difference between C_{u_relax} of the oracle NPE and the reference C ≤ 0.10 (median) (b) C_ODI ≥ 0.9 (c) median C2ST accuracy ≤ 0.65. **On failure S4 as a whole fails** (meaning that the judge is inventing information that algebraically does not exist).
### (iii) Prior sensitivity — labels
- **Produced from card data only** (ZZZg-3). ×2 equals the training prior, so it is the **posterior without reweighting** · ×0.5 is an **additional reweighting** of the main-prior posterior («reweighting impossible» if fewer than 200 samples remain). Produced for each of the nine k; a (pair · direction) in which the classification of the worst k differs from the main prior is «prior-dependent» · not written as «identified». Generator assumption: if the classifications of the nine k split, «assumption-dependent». Not an overall verdict criterion (labels and proportions are reported).
### (iv) Fidelity
- From the conditional posterior at the true k (ZZZg-1), the inclusion rates of the ensemble and the individual models are reported side by side · **the ensemble must pass (i)** · the C2ST of (ii) also serves as a local fidelity check.
### (v) Misspecification score — fixed before measurement
- Score (changed by ZZZg-4): k is unknown in real data, so **−log[(1/9) Σ_k q(x | a, k)]** — the density of the uniform mixture over the nine k of the q(x | a) flow (same simulation · k is part of condition a). ## 13. Implementation details — not in the specification · fixed before results [design]
1. **Posterior samples (changed by ZZZg-1):** the conditional posterior of one generator assumption k = uniform mixture of the five in the ensemble (the same number drawn per model · only the k cell of condition a is changed — the «true k» of the test is the condition of the case as it is). Samples outside the training support are discarded, and more are drawn until the samples inside the main prior support (§3-2) reach the target (§13 14) (at most 20 times per case). If the target is not reached, «reweighting impossible». The **reference uniform mixture** is the main-support samples of the nine k, pooled with weights «main-support proportion / number of samples» (= the uniform mixture of the nine training-prior posteriors reweighted to the main prior · old definition) — not used for verdict · classification.
2. **u_relax:** at tissue_A of that case, the amount δ by which the apparent signal fractions (ν′, f′_iso) of the relaxation centre (T2_in 82 · r 1.6 · T2_iso 1000 · T1_tissue 1,100 · scenario iii) change from acquisition A to B — u_relax = (δν′, δf′_iso, 0)/|δ| · u_⊥ = (−u₂, u₁, 0). If |δ| < 1e-6 (matched pair) it is not defined.
3. **Var_prior(uᵀΔ) = uᵀ Cov_prior(Δ) u** — Cov_prior(Δ) is computed once from 10⁶ samples of the main prior (with truncation · §3-2) (the variance is a quadratic form in u, so this is exact for all u).
4. **Design-level C:** for each case of that cell · stratum, c_u per k is computed, then the median per k — the card · map classify by the worst value of the nine (with the median) · P1 – P5 give the verdict by the median of the nine per-k stratum medians. If even one k is «reweighting impossible», that cell is «reweighting impossible».
5. **Stratum failure in calibration (§7 i):** if even one of the twelve cells (three components × four levels) of a stratum is below the threshold, that stratum fails.
6. **Inclusion rate of individual models (§7 iv):** computed from the conditional posterior samples at the true k split by model (about 200 per model) — reporting only.
7. **Oracle (§7 ii):** the oracle NPE and the reference posterior are both computed under the condition a of the case (including the true generator assumption — not mixed). Nuisance variables of the reference posterior: relaxation of A (§3-3 range) · random z · z′ of L1 (§3-3).
   **Reference posterior MCMC (ZZZg-2):** state 12 dimensions = w (the six noise-free apparent ν′ · f′_iso · ODI of sites A · B) · relaxation of A (T2_in · r · c) · z (2) · z′ (1) — z · z′ enter the likelihood only in L1 and otherwise run as in the prior. Potential = exact Gaussian likelihood N(observation; w, 0.005²) + main prior of θ\* (truncated uniform → support indicator) + relaxation uniform · z normal (|z|² ≤ 4 truncation) · z′ normal (clipped at ±2 in the likelihood — same generating process as `s4cfg.relax_B`) + Jacobian log|∂θ\*/∂w| of the change of variables w → θ\*. θ\* is obtained by inverting the apparent fractions: ν = ν′a_en / (ν′a_en + (1 − ν′)a_in) · f = f′A / (f′A + (1 − f′)a_iso) · A = νa_in + (1 − ν)a_en [algebra] — in w coordinates the likelihood is nearly axis-aligned, so coordinate-wise slice sampling mixes well (same posterior · only the coordinates are changed). For the sampler, this potential is given to `SliceSamplerVectorized` of sbi 0.27.0 in the lock (the one called by `slice_np_vectorized` of MCMCPosterior) — a batch of cases (25 cases = 100 chains) is run at once. The discarded 500 are the width-tuning phase of the sampler. Convergence diagnostics (numpy · scipy): rank-normalized split R̂ (larger of bulk · folded) · bulk ESS of rank-normalized split chains (mean between-chain autocovariance · Geyer initial monotone sequence) [design — the formulas are those of Vehtari et al. 2021 · bibliographic details not checked]. Check (2026-10-25 · 25 cases with a new seed, not the actual reference cases): all converged (R̂ ≤ 1.002 · bulk ESS minimum 2,515) · 25 cases 87 s. C2ST on the six dimensions of θ\* · 1,000 samples on each side · random forest (sbi default) · C_ODI of (b) is the median over all oracle reference cases.
8. **Misspecification threshold (§7 v):** the score is −log[(1/9) Σ_k q(x | a, k)] (computed by log-sum-exp · ZZZg-4). For each stratum of the test data, the cases are split in half; the 99 percentile of the first half is the threshold, and the false-alarm rate is measured on the second half. The stratum of an injection case is set by the acquisition kind («both independent» is TE+TR if the design is the same · TE+TR+design if different).
9. **P3:** matched pairs have no u_relax, so the C of matched-pair L0 is measured along u_ref, the mean direction of u_relax of the L0 test cases with only TR differing (once over the whole test data · algebra) — C_{u_relax}(TR only · L0) ≤ C_{u_ref}(match · L0) − 0.2 (on both sides the median of the stratum medians of the nine k).
10. **P5:** for matched pairs, Δσ = √max(σ²_NDI(L1) − σ²_NDI(L0), 0) is computed from the L1 cases with ρ = 0, and it is checked whether it is within ±50 % of the expected value 0.397 × s_rms (root mean square of s_T2 of those cases — the apparent NDI SD of X13 (a) is proportional to CV: 0.0059/0.015 ≈ 0.0119/0.03 ≈ 0.0159/0.04 ≈ 0.397).
11. **P6:** in strata where TE differs (TE only · TE+TR · TE+TR+design), the proportion of cases in which the sign of the NDI component of u_relax, computed with the relaxation multiplied by the L3 multipliers instead of the relaxation centre, is opposite to that of L0 (relaxation centre) > 0.5.
12. **Linear baseline (§6):** for each cell of the card · map (observations M = 100 · θ\* from the main prior · nuisance variables and the conditions within the cell remain random), linear regression of x on θ\* (with intercept) → J · residual covariance R (contains the share of the nuisance variables — equivalent to the Schur complement) → posterior covariance (Jᵀ R⁻¹ J + Cov_prior(θ\*)⁻¹)⁻¹ (Cov_prior(θ\*) from 10⁶ samples of the main prior) → C_u^lin.
13. **Interpolation error of the compartment signal table:** at grid points equal to synth.Proto within float32 rounding (3e-8), and between grid points (midpoints) at most 8.8e-4 (signal units — about 5 % of the smallest noise σ = 1/60) — checked 2026-10-24 (the values are not simulation results).

14. **Number of samples per k (after ZZZg-1 · pre-results):** test — true k 1,000 (inclusion · rank · TARP) + each of the nine k 250 (c · σ — P1 – P5 · only the variance is used, so relative SE ≈ 9 % · stratum median from about 1,000 cases) · card — 2,000 per k (so that at least 200 samples remain after ×0.5 reweighting — about 1/8 – 1/4 of the main-support samples) · map — 1,000 per k (no sensitivity) · injection — true k 1,000. Cost is proportional to the number of samples (check 2026-10-25: sampling is 98 % of the time). The rule of §10-1 «estimate from the first 2 % · reduce if above 4 hours» stands unchanged.

## Appendix A. V4 — core rules of the travelling-heads measurement calibration test (relay ZZZo-2 · author decision D-o3 · fixed before the S4 results were opened)
V4 applies the frozen judge (S4 · weights 85eb8b0) to the ON-Harmony travelling heads measurement. The true tissue difference is 0, so it checks whether the posterior intervals cover 0 at the nominal rate. **S4 is not trained again.** Measurement computation is done only after the commit of the fixed analysis document of the measurement — only the rules are written here.
- **(a) Identity of processing:** the measurement is processed in the same way as the simulator observation model — fitted per voxel with F2 (AMICO · same settings as S4 · §3-7), then averaged over the ROI. The ROI set · session list are the same as in the fixed analysis document.
- **(b) N_eff:** not estimated from the measurement. The verdict uses N_eff = 50 (main cell of the card), and 25 · 100 are reported with it as sensitivity.
- **(c) Cells to test — most important:** the coverage rate is counted only in cells (acquisition type × component) classified as **«identified»** in the S4 results (classification rule as in this specification — worst k · median alongside). Cells not identified are written separately as «coverage — no information» and are **not counted as passes** (this prevents a wide interval from covering 0 and passing for free).
- **(d) ROI unit · dependence · threshold:** evaluation ROI unit = scanner pair × ROI × component · nominal 90 % central credible interval. Uncertainty of the coverage rate comes from a subject-level cluster bootstrap, and scanner sharing is examined together with a scanner-level permutation. **The threshold is set from the model itself** — the **5 percentile** of the distribution made by drawing the coverage rate many times from the S4 test data with the same number of ROI units · same cluster structure. This computation follows this rule after the S4 results commit. If it cannot be done, a fallback threshold of 0.80 (design value · rationale is the review session's proposal) is used and that fact is written down.
- **(e) Blinded-status labels:** ODI per pair — blinded confirmation · NDI · FISO — **unblinded check** (the difference was seen in the old analysis · not written as «confirmation»).
- **(f) Interpretation — set in advance:** pass → «in this design family · this type, scanner effects not in the model did not sway the verdict» · fail → «no verdict possible for this type on real data» · the G2 – G4 candidates are written in the coverage table · S4 itself fails the criteria (§11) → V4 is not done and only the algebraic card is used for the measurement.
- **(g) No changes:** this rule is not changed after the S4 results are opened. Only parts that are technically impossible are recorded as deviations.
